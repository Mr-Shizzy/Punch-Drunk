POW: Recompiled!!

Set up POW: Recompiled!!

Bring your own copy: the port is built on this computer from your copy of the game. No game data is included.

  1. 1Build tools

      Install them with:

      
              
      
              
      The launcher downloads these into its own folder; nothing else on your PC changes. The Visual C++ runtime is Microsoft's own installer, so Windows asks for permission. The compiler comes during the build.

      2Your disc
      
            
      
              
      
                
      Your Punch-Out!! (Wii, USA) disc. ISO, WBFS or RVZ.
      
                
      No disc chosen
      
                
              
      
              
      
                
      Your Doc Louis's Punch-Out!! (WiiWare, USA) WAD, a backup of your own copy.
      
                
      No WAD chosen
      
                
      
                  
      To unpack it, the launcher needs the Wii common key from your own console (in the keys.bin of your BootMii backup). It's used once to unpack the WAD and is never saved.

    Controls
    
        
    Play with the keyboard or a controller; the game treats both like a Wii Remote held sideways. The mouse moves the pointer in menus: left click chooses, right click goes back. These controls are for both games.
    
        
    
          
          
          
        
    
        
    
        
        
    
        
        
    
          
    In a fightWii RemoteKeyboardController
    
          
        
    
        
    Click a box to change it, then press the key, mouse button or controller button. The left stick always moves too. In menus, a controller's A chooses and B goes back; on the keyboard, your left punch and right punch keys do. The bar at the bottom of menus shows them. 
    
        
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
        
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    
        
    Gameplay
    
        
    Archipelago is on, so the difficulty stays at Normal.
    
        
    
          
          
          
        
    
        
    Punch-Out!!'s career fights only. Also in the game: Options → Difficulty. Everything else on this page is for both games.
    
        
      
    
      
    
        
    Graphics
    
        
    These are the same settings as the game's own menu: press F10 in the game and open Graphics. Changing them either place changes both.
    
        
    
          Aspect ratio
          
    
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
        
    
          Render resolution
          
    
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
        
    
          Display mode
